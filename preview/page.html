Delivery & Return

Orders placed before 4pm are dispatched the same working day. Inside Dhaka, delivery is usually next day; outside Dhaka it takes two to three days depending on the courier.

Returns

You can return an unopened product within seven days of delivery. Opened products can be returned only if the item is faulty on arrival.

  1. Message us with your order number
  2. Pack the item with everything it came with
  3. Hand it to the courier we arrange

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days of the item reaching our warehouse.
